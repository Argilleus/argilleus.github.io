Argilleus Music Library

Free sheet music, carefully edited.

Download sheet music of selected works of western classical music in modern, clean editions, available as .pdf files and .mscz files.

The International Music Score Library Project/Petrucci Music Library (imslp.org) is an amazing project and a great resource for musicians, That I've used extensively and that you should definetely support. I am quite aware that Argilleus will never reach the enormous size of imslp, and honestly, the projects have different goals. However, the scores available at imslp are most often scans of physical editions. Sometimes the resolution is not the best, sometimes the file size is too big, sometimes they're just not as practical or just don't look that good. The goal of the Argilleus Music Library is to provide modern, clean editions of classical pieces. The editions presented here are digital renders, not scans of physical editions, which makes them look a lot better on screens and makes them ideal for printing. The musical works published on this website are believed to be in the public domain. The editions have been newly engraved by Argilleus to provide clean, modern sheet music suitable for reading on screen and printing.
