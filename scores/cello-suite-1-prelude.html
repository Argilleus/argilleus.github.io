About the Work

Cello Suite No. 1 in G Major, BWV 1007 is the first of Johann Sebastian Bach's six suites for unaccompanied cello, composed during his years at the court of Köthen. The suite opens with the celebrated Prelude, one of the most recognizable works in the cello repertoire, and continues with a sequence of Baroque dance movements: Allemande, Courante, Sarabande, Menuets I & II, and Gigue. Renowned for its elegance, clarity, and expressive depth, it remains a cornerstone of the solo cello literature.
