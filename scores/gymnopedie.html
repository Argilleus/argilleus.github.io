About the Work

Gymnopédie No. 1 is the first of three Gymnopédies composed by Erik Satie in 1888. Marked Lent et douloureux, the work is characterized by its serene melody, gentle harmonic language, and understated rhythmic flow. Its simplicity and quiet introspection have made it one of Satie's most celebrated compositions and a landmark of the early modern piano repertoire.
