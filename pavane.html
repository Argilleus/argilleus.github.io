About the Work

Pavane pour une infante défunte is one of Maurice Ravel's earliest and most beloved piano works, composed in 1899. Written in the style of a Renaissance pavane, the piece is not intended as a lament for an actual princess, but rather as an evocation of the graceful courtly dances of a distant past. Its elegant melody, subtle harmonies, and refined lyricism have made it a staple of the piano repertoire and one of Ravel's most frequently performed works.
