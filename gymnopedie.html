About the Work

Gymnopédie No. 1 is one of Erik Satie's most celebrated piano works, composed in 1888. Its quiet atmosphere, flowing rhythm, and restrained harmonic language have made it one of the defining works of the late nineteenth-century piano repertoire. Today it remains among Satie's best-known compositions and is regularly performed and recorded.
